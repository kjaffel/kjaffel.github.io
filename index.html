About me

I am an experimental particle physicist in the CMS Collaboration at the LHC, based at CERN and at the KBFI/NICPB institute in Tallinn. I search for physics beyond the Standard Model through the extended Higgs sector and the Higgs boson self-coupling, and I develop machine-learning techniques to make these searches more sensitive.

I did my Ph.D. at UCLouvain (Belgium) on the search for 2HDM neutral Higgs bosons in ℓ⁺ℓ⁻bb̄ final states, then worked as a postdoc at USTC (China) while based at CERN. Since October 2025 I am a postdoc at KBFI.

Beyond my own analyses, I convene the CMS Monte Carlo & Interpretation group, co-convene the Extended Higgs Sector subgroup of the LHC Higgs Working Group, and help develop Bamboo, an analysis framework for ROOT RDataFrame.

Away from the collider I love to travel, take photographs and read. You can find some of that in Free time.

What I am working on

  • Run 3 search for X → YH → 4b in boosted topologies
  • Run 3 searches for HH and HHH production
  • Heavy Higgs bosons in extended scalar sectors (2HDM)
  • CMS analysis frameworks: Bamboo and ColumnFlow
